The city is dark

The tired streets in sleepy delirium

Burns...

The eyes of the city burn with smoke.

The city guards have lit a fire

With the bushes of ignorance

The fire that they have said...

This is the eternal light...

The eternal fire is the awakened sun...

.... Believe the lie that must be told

The dagger of suffering.

The soul, in the sense that

We build with suffering... burns

Except for the heavy hearts of our murderers

Whatever we throw into the fire burns.

It is the season of humiliation...

A season that means the cry of chaos...

The buds wither and die in the coldness of hearts

And.... The flower of a cry does not grow on the lips.

It is the season of humiliation...

Whoever has a lock on his lips

His hands are fruitful horns.

The city.... has become a thirsty plain
