From the side of the plain
From the side of the mountain...
From the clear blue of the old, glorious...
I came to the city of smoke...
I came to the city full of noise without a song...
No... I came to the city without hope, tired and hunched..... Life was passing by...
It was sighing...
The snake of fatigue... my hand-wringing...
My eyes seeing...
It was biting my feet on the path of love...
In such a blue sky... in such a sunset of sadness...
In such a drunkenness, tired and hunched...
Wow, if it weren't for your imagination
There wouldn't be the electricity of your clear spring.
Wow, if it weren't for your voice... There wouldn't be your secret
There wouldn't be your wounds
There wouldn't be your open arms
In the frightened shepherd of the city...
A tired man was longing for the moon
Under the shadows of sadness, your alley was drowning in sighs...
In the sunset of these stars...
In the wounded voice of these minarets...
In the distant sound
In the sound of songless iron volumes.....
Life was ruined.
The city was a black curtain
Love on you was a beacon.... It was a flame to erase every sin...
