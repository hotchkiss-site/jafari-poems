Translator’s note

The Persian here is a camera capture of a printed page, and the OCR is corrupt in about a third of its lines; it is kept exactly as captured, since it is the only record we have. Four readings are safe enough to stand: تو در تو is the idiom "fold within fold," not the pronoun تو, "your" (an earlier English had "your maze of blackness"); کی ترا شناختی؟ must be شناختم, since an object ترا cannot take a second-person verb — "when did I come to know you?"; میو تو آواز خواندم is من با تو آواز خواندم, "I sang with you," which the two other آواز خواندم lines support; and نیر is نیز, "also." The doubled کاشی باران باریا — کاش باران ببارد is one damaged line and its correction, so only the correction is rendered. Two conjectures are attractive but stay out of the text: آواز خستهٔ مرا زانی کرد may be جوانی کرد, "made my tired song young"; and تمام شب تابی may be تمام شب‌تابی, "all the firefly-glow," rather than "all night, a glimmer." The previous English confidently rendered the corrupt lines as "the spinning sweet breads," "Persian and the green line," and "You were like a brazier"; none of those readings can be got out of the letters on the page, and they have been withdrawn. The poem needs the printed page, not a better guess.
